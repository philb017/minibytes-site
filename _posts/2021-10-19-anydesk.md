Silent install, suitable for pushing out via PsExec.

**Install**

```powershell
AnyDesk.exe --install "%programfiles%\AnyDesk" --start-with-win --silent --create-shortcuts
```

**Get the AnyDesk ID after install**

```powershell
"%programfiles%\AnyDesk\AnyDesk.exe" --get-id
```
